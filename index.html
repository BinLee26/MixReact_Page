Physics-Based Reactive Humanoid Control from Multimodal Cues
via a Discrete–Continuous Mixture of Transformers

Bin Li* 1Xing Zhu* 1Jingya Wang† 1,2

1 ShanghaiTech University2 InstAdapt

* Equal contribution.   † Corresponding author.

Demo

Abstract

Humanoid agents in everyday environments are expected to react to diverse cues as humans do, with semantically appropriate and physically executable behaviors. Existing cue-driven reaction generation methods support only a narrow range of cue modalities and are limited to purely kinematic generation, leaving the resulting reactions susceptible to physical artifacts. To this end, we present MixReact, the first unified framework for physics-based reactive humanoid control from text, audio, and video cues, integrating a Discrete–Continuous Mixture of Transformers (DisCo-MoT) for state prediction with a History-aware Action Transformer (HAT) for physical execution. DisCo-MoT couples a discrete masked-modeling branch with a continuous autoregressive diffusion branch via one-way mixture attention within a MoT architecture. Guidance from discrete context mitigates collapse into averaged behaviors in one-to-many reaction generation, while direct prediction in continuous state space bypasses the decoding bottleneck of discrete representations. HAT then maps the predicted next state and observed execution history to executable actions, enabling robust closed-loop control in physics-based simulation. Experiments across all three modalities demonstrate consistent improvements over the baselines in fidelity, diversity, and physical consistency, while ablations validate the complementary contributions of the components. Our code will be released to facilitate future research.

Motivation

Everyday cues.
More than one response.

“Congratulations!” may elicit a bow, a handshake, or a celebratory jump. MixReact models these distinct choices and executes reactions in physics-based simulation.

Method

Plan globally. Adapt continuously.

DisCo-MoT unites discrete reaction planning and continuous state prediction. HAT closes the loop with physical execution.

Persistent reaction context

A complete discrete plan maintains behavioral structure across prediction windows.

Fine-grained state prediction

One-way attention guides continuous denoising while protecting discrete representations.

History-aware control

HAT uses predicted states and observed history to adapt actions during execution.
